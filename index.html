Towers

How to use

Set up puzzle: pick a size, click a clue square around the edge (or a grid cell, for a pre-filled value) and type a number. 0 or Backspace clears it.

Solve: click a cell and type a number. Press N or the ✎ button to switch to notes, or hold Shift while typing to toggle a note. Arrow keys move, ⌘/Ctrl+Z undoes.

Fill candidates writes, into every empty cell, each value that fits at least one valid completion of its row and of its column, given the numbers you've placed and the clues. After that, it's up to you to eliminate. Mark impossible notes strikes through notes that this check rules out, without changing them.

A clue turns green once its line is full and correct, red if it's full and wrong. A row or column gets a red outline when no arrangement of it can satisfy its clues anymore.
